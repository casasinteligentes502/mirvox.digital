How do I request a quote?
Add the products, enter desired quantities and send your request via WhatsApp. We confirm pricing and stock before any payment.
Are the prices final?
This is a demonstration catalog without final prices. All offers, availability and shipping charges must be confirmed directly with MIRVOX.
How can I check bulb compatibility?
Check the vehicle manual or share make, model, year and part reference. Bulb base, voltage and manufacturer specifications must also be verified.
Do you quote for repair shops and resellers?
You may request volume quotes for workshops and retailers. Wholesale terms depend on quantities and availability.
Do you ship within Guatemala?
We can review your location and quote available delivery options. Coverage, timelines and fees are confirmed per order.
Can I pay on this website?
No. This website prepares quote requests; it does not charge cards or confirm purchases automatically.
